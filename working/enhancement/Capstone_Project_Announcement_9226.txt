Capstone Project Announcement for Group 9226
Data Analytics for AI Business

Dear Students,
Your capstone project will bring together business analytics, Power BI and Artificial Intelligence to
support an executive decision in your assigned business setting. Each team will work as an Executive
Analytics Advisory Team and develop an integrated dashboard, presentation and analytical report.
The project materials will be made available through Moodle. Each team package comprises:
• The assigned enterprise dataset in CSV format.
• The team project title, business scenario and reference publication.
• The supporting analytics workbook with dataset metadata and analytical guidance.
• The official team allocation and capstone submission instructions.
Read the Final Capstone Project Description for Group 9226 before starting. It sets out the learning
outcomes, analytical scope, deliverables, assessment criteria, submission requirements and presentation
expectations.
Submission requirements
• Executive Power BI Dashboard (.pbix).
• Executive Presentation (.pptx), recommended 12-15 slides.
• Comprehensive Analytical Report (.pdf), recommended 20-25 pages.
Submit one integrated package per team through Moodle. A maximum of three files may be uploaded;
use the required formats and include supporting model evidence and AI disclosure within the

Team Preparation and Executive Expectations
Begin by reviewing the assigned business case, dataset definitions and analytical grain. Divide
responsibilities across governance, exploratory analysis, Power BI development, AI evaluation, executive
recommendations and reporting. Keep a shared work plan and ensure every team member understands
the integrated result.
The supplied datasets and reference publications provide a starting point. Your team must verify the
measures, conduct its own analysis, build a functioning Power BI dashboard and present its own
evidence-based conclusions. Copying the reference publication does not demonstrate completion of the
capstone.
All findings must be traceable to your assigned dataset. Distinguish observed synthetic results from
proposed actions, test selected AI methods against a suitable baseline, and report weaknesses as clearly
as strengths. A model that adds no value is a valid analytical finding when supported by a sound
evaluation.
Your executive briefing should explain the business problem, the most important findings, the decision
implications and the limits of the evidence. Use clear visuals and practical recommendations. Disclose AI
assistance and describe how your team validated the final work.
Raise project questions during class sessions or through the official course communication channels. The
instructors will communicate any changes to dates, allocation or assessment through Moodle.
We look forward to reviewing your analytical work and executive presentations.
